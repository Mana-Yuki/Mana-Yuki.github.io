-~- Games -~-

A showcase of the games I've worked on!

Grandma Gretchin's Cookie Hustle

A short arcade game about leading grandma gretchin around town to sell her cookies.

Made in 48-hours for the GAMED October 2026 Game Jam.

Forecaster

A rogue-like deckbuilder where the current weather and forecast impacts your cards.

Made in 48-hours for the GAMED April 2026 Game Jam.

Knife to a Gunfight

An action game where you have to get close and eliminate all your enemies using your knife, but everyone else has guns!

Made in 48-hours for the GAMED November 2024 Game Jam.

Eugor

A topdown wave survival game where taking damage gives you "upgrades".

Made in 48-hours for the GAMED November 2025 Game Jam.
